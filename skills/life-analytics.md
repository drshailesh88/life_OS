### 5. Correlation Strength Calculation

**Simple Method:**

```
For two variables (e.g., Sleep Hours vs Energy):

1. Group data:
   - Sleep <7hrs → Energy levels
   - Sleep 7+hrs → Energy levels

2. Compare averages:
   - <7hrs: Energy avg 2.5/5
   - 7+hrs: Energy avg 3.8/5
   - Difference: +1.3 points (52% improvement)

3. Assess significance:
   - Large sample (30+ days): High confidence
   - Small sample (<10 days): Low confidence
   - Consistent pattern: High confidence
   - Noisy pattern: Medium confidence
```

**Statistical (Advanced):**
- Pearson correlation coefficient
- R-squared values
- P-values for significance
