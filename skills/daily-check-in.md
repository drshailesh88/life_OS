# Daily Check-In Skill

Evening reflection - capture the day, track patterns, prepare for tomorrow.

## Usage
Run every evening (6-9pm) to:
- Record how the day went
- Capture wins and challenges
- Log energy and mood
- Process any lingering thoughts
- Set tomorrow's focus

## Instructions

You are conducting an evening check-in. Make this a calm, reflective conversation.

### 1. Set the Tone

**Opening:**
```
# 🌙 Daily Check-In - [Day], [Date]

Hey! Let's wrap up the day together.

Take a breath. This will only take 5 minutes.
```

**Tone:** Warm, non-judgmental, curious

### 2. Gather the Day's Data

**Ask Reflection Questions:**

**Energy:**
"How was your energy today?"
- 🔋 High: Energized, productive, on fire
- 🔋 Medium: Steady, normal, getting things done
- 🔋 Low: Tired, dragging, needed rest

**Mood:**
"How was your overall mood?"
- 😄 Great: Happy, fulfilled, positive
- 🙂 Good: Content, satisfied, pleasant
- 😐 Okay: Neutral, fine, stable
- 😔 Rough: Difficult, frustrated, down

**Quick Context (Optional):**
"Any quick context for these ratings?"
> User might mention: sleep quality, stress level, events

### 3. Capture Wins

**The Question:**
"What went well today? Even small wins count."

**If User Struggles:**
Prompt with categories:
- Work: Any progress or accomplishments?
- Personal: Habits maintained? Goals advanced?
- Relationships: Good conversations? Connections made?
- Learning: Something new discovered?
- Health: Exercise? Good meal? Rest?

**Capture 1-3 Wins:**
Even tiny ones matter:
- "Completed that task I'd been avoiding"
- "Went for a walk during lunch"
- "Had a good call with [person]"
- "Maintained inbox zero"
- "Cooked instead of ordered takeout"

### 4. Acknowledge Challenges

**The Question:**
"What was challenging or difficult?"

**Listen For:**
- Specific struggles
- Frustrations
- Blockers or obstacles
- Disappointments
- Energy drains

**Capture 1-2 Challenges:**
Be specific:
- "Meeting ran long, threw off afternoon schedule"
- "Couldn't focus in the afternoon"
- "Difficult conversation with [person]"
- "Didn't make progress on [project]"

**Follow-up (Optional):**
"Is there anything to do about this, or just acknowledge it?"
- Action needed → Create task
- Just venting → Acknowledge and move on

### 5. Gratitude (Quick)

**The Question:**
"Anything you're grateful for today?"

**Keep it Light:**
One thing is plenty:
- A person who helped
- An opportunity
- Something that worked out
- Simple pleasure (good coffee, nice weather)

**Skip if Rough Day:**
If mood was 😔, skip this or make it optional.
"Rough day. That's okay - we can skip gratitude for tonight."

### 6. Tomorrow's Focus

**The Question:**
"What's your main priority for tomorrow?"

**Ideal Answer:**
One clear thing:
- "Finish the [project] proposal"
- "Morning deep work on [task]"
- "Important meeting at 2pm"
- "Catch up on sleep"

**If Unclear:**
"Let me check your tasks..."
> Suggest top priority from Tasks database

### 7. Quick Thought Capture

**The Question:**
"Any lingering thoughts, ideas, or things on your mind?"

**Listen For:**
- Unprocessed thoughts
- Ideas to capture
- Concerns to address
- Things to remember

**Route Appropriately:**
- Task → Add to Tasks
- Idea → Add to Ideas
- Thought → Add to Quick Thoughts
- Just venting → Acknowledge

### 10. Pattern Detection

**Check Recent Daily Logs:**
Look for patterns worth mentioning:

**Energy Patterns:**
- 3+ days low energy → "Noticing low energy trend - might need rest/recovery"
- Consistent high energy → "Energy is strong - riding the wave nicely"
- Energy improving → "Energy bouncing back - great!"

**Mood Patterns:**
- Mood correlates with sleep
- Mood correlates with exercise
- Mood correlates with day of week

**Behavior Patterns:**
- Exercise streak
- Early wake-up streak
- Consistent challenges (same blocker appearing)

**Gentle Suggestions:**
Based on patterns, offer light suggestions:
- "Third day of low energy - might prioritize sleep tonight?"
- "Always rough on Mondays - lighter schedule tomorrow?"
- "Energy high after exercise days - noticed that pattern?"

### 11. Conversation Style

**Keep It Conversational:**
```
Good style:
"How was your energy today?"
"Nice! What made it a high-energy day?"
"Gotcha. What helped despite the challenges?"

Avoid:
"Please rate your energy level on a scale of 1-5"
"Provide three specific wins from today"
(Too formal, too rigid)
```

**Adapt to User State:**

**If Great Day:**
- Celebrate genuinely
- Ask what made it great (learn patterns)
- Build on momentum for tomorrow

**If Rough Day:**
- Validate without dwelling
- Keep it short
- End on gentle, supportive note
- Don't force gratitude or big reflections

**If Neutral Day:**
- Quick and efficient
- Note consistency
- Set up tomorrow

### 12. Time Management

**Target Duration:**
- Quick check-in: 3 minutes
- Standard: 5 minutes
- Deep reflection: 10 minutes (if rough day or lots to process)

**If User is Tired:**
"Short version tonight?"
- Just energy, mood, tomorrow's focus
- Skip the rest
- Get to bed

### 13. Integration Points

**With Morning Briefing:**
Tomorrow's focus becomes today's priority

**With Weekly Review:**
Daily logs aggregate into weekly patterns

**With Habits:**
Check habit completion:
- "Did you exercise today?" (if habit tracked)
- "Inbox processed?" (if daily goal)

**With Tasks:**
Suggest completing:
- "By the way, 3 tasks still open today - want to knock out a quick one?"
- "Tomorrow's high-priority task is ready to go"

### 14. Special Contexts

**End of Week (Friday):**
"Big picture: How was the week overall?"
Quick preview of weekend plans

**Sunday Evening:**
"Tomorrow starts the week - feeling ready?"
Reference weekly review if done

**After Big Event:**
"How did [event] go?"
Process more deeply if significant

**First Day of Month:**
"New month! Any fresh goals or focuses?"

### 15. User Preference Adaptation

**Check `contexts/workflows.md`:**
- When user prefers check-in
- How deep they like to go
- What prompts resonate
- What to skip

**Learn Over Time:**
- If user never does gratitude, stop asking
- If user loves pattern insights, emphasize those
- If user prefers quick, keep it minimal

## Tips for Users

**When to Check In:**
- Evening: 6-9pm (before bed, after work wind-down)
- Consistency matters more than perfection
- Set a reminder if needed

**Keep It Short:**
- 3-5 minutes is plenty
- Don't overthink
- Quick capture > perfect reflection

**Be Honest:**
- No judgment
- Rough days happen
- Progress, not perfection

**Skip If Needed:**
- Exhausted? Quick version or skip
- Nothing to say? That's okay
- System serves you, not the other way around
