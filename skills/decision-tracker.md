# Decision Tracker Skill

Track major decisions, learn from outcomes, and improve decision quality over time.

## Usage

**Track a New Decision:**
```
"I'm deciding whether to [decision]"
"Help me think through [decision]"
"Track this decision: [context]"
```

**Review Past Decisions:**
```
"Show me my decisions from last quarter"
"What have I learned from past decisions?"
"Review my decision about [topic]"
```

**Decision Quality Analysis:**
```
"How good are my decisions?"
"What's my decision-making track record?"
"Analyze my decision patterns"
```

## Instructions

You help users make better decisions through systematic tracking and analysis.

### 1. Decision Framework

Every decision should capture:

**CONTEXT:**
- What's the decision?
- Why now?
- What led to this point?
- What's at stake?

**OPTIONS:**
- What are ALL the options?
- What's the status quo (doing nothing)?
- What's the bold move?
- What's the middle ground?

**CRITERIA:**
- What matters most?
- How will you know if it was right?
- What's your timeframe?
- What are the risks?

**ANALYSIS:**
- Pros/cons of each option
- Reversibility (can you undo it?)
- Opportunity cost
- Alignment with values/goals

**DECISION:**
- What did you choose?
- Why this over others?
- Confidence level (1-10)
- Expected outcome

**FOLLOW-UP:**
- Check-in date (review the outcome)
- What would change your mind?
- What will you measure?

### 3. Decision Process

**Step 1: Capture the Decision**

```markdown
User: "I'm deciding whether to take the new job offer"

Let me help you think through this systematically.

# Decision: Job Offer

## Context

**What:** Offer from [Company] for [Role]
**Timeline:** Decision needed by [Date]
**Stakes:** Career direction, income, lifestyle

Let me ask some clarifying questions:

1. **What's driving this decision?**
   - Unhappy in current role?
   - Better opportunity?
   - External pressure?

2. **What matters most to you?**
   - Compensation?
   - Growth potential?
   - Work-life balance?
   - Mission/impact?

3. **What are you optimizing for?**
   - Short-term (1-2 years)?
   - Long-term (5+ years)?
   - Specific skill development?

[Continue dialogue to understand fully]
```

**Step 2: Explore ALL Options**

```markdown
## Options

Let's map out ALL your options (not just the obvious ones):

**Option 1: Take the New Job**
- Salary: [amount]
- Role: [description]
- Company: [info]
- Location: [where]
- Start date: [when]

**Option 2: Stay at Current Job**
- Status quo
- Known quantity
- Existing relationships
- Current trajectory

**Option 3: Negotiate Counter-Offer**
- Leverage new offer
- Get raise/promotion at current company
- Stay with familiar
- Risk: May damage relationships

**Option 4: Keep Looking**
- Decline this offer
- Continue search
- Wait for better fit
- Risk: Opportunity cost

**Option 5: [Other Options]**
- Freelance/consulting?
- Start own business?
- Sabbatical/break?
- Pivot to different field?

Which options resonate? Any I'm missing?
```

**Step 3: Define Criteria**

```markdown
## Decision Criteria

Based on your values (from contexts/profile.md), let's rank what matters:

**Your Top Priorities (ranked):**
1. **Learning & Growth** (10/10 importance)
   - Will this role stretch you?
   - Clear path to advancement?
   - Skill development?

2. **Compensation** (9/10 importance)
   - Total comp (salary + equity + benefits)?
   - Vs. cost of living adjustment?
   - Financial goals impact?

3. **Work-Life Balance** (8/10 importance)
   - Hours expected?
   - Flexibility?
   - Commute/remote?

4. **Mission Alignment** (7/10 importance)
   - Do you believe in the product?
   - Company values match yours?
   - Proud to work there?

5. **Team & Culture** (8/10 importance)
   - Met the team?
   - Manager quality?
   - Culture fit?

**Minimum Requirements (deal-breakers):**
- ❌ Won't take if: [criteria]
- ✅ Must have: [criteria]
```

**Scores:**
- Learning & Growth: 4/10 ❌
- Compensation: 6/10 ⚠️
- Work-Life Balance: 10/10 ✅
- Mission Alignment: 5/10 ⚠️
- Team & Culture: 9/10 ✅

**Overall: 34/50 (68%)**

---

### Option 3: Counter-Offer

[Similar analysis...]

---

### Option 4: Keep Looking

[Similar analysis...]

---

## Key Insights

**Trade-offs:**
- New job: Growth & Money ↔ Work-Life Balance
- Current job: Comfort ↔ Stagnation

**Reversibility:**

**Alignment with Goals:**

Good luck! 🚀

### 8. Advanced Features

**Pre-Mortems:**
```
"Let's do a pre-mortem on [decision]"

Imagine it's 12 months from now and this decision failed spectacularly.

What went wrong?
- [Potential failure mode 1]
- [Potential failure mode 2]
- [Potential failure mode 3]

How can you prevent each?
- [Mitigation strategy]
```

**Regret Minimization:**
```
"Help me think about regret for [decision]"

Imagine you're 80 years old, looking back.

Will you regret:
- DOING this? [Why?]
- NOT doing this? [Why?]

Which regret is bigger?
[Usually: Regret of inaction > regret of action]
```

**Second-Order Effects:**
```
"What are the second-order effects of [decision]?"

First-order: [Direct consequence]

Second-order: [What does that lead to?]
- [Effect on relationships]
- [Effect on future options]
- [Effect on identity]

Third-order: [And then what?]
```

**Track Your Calibration:**

```

- How often are you right? [Should be ~80%]
- If you're right 95% of time: You're overconfident
- If you're right 60% of time: You're underconfident

Good calibration = confidence matches reality
```
