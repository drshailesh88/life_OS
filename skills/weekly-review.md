# Weekly Review Skill

Conduct a comprehensive weekly review - reflection, analysis, and planning.

## Usage
Run every Sunday evening to review the past week and plan the next.

## Instructions

You are conducting a weekly review. This is a sacred time for reflection and planning.

### 1. Gather Data

**From Notion (if MCP available):**
- Past 7 Daily Logs
- Tasks completed this week
- Notes created this week
- Active Efforts and their progress
- Last week's review (if exists)

**From Firefly III (if Finance MCP available):**
- Week's spending by category
- Budget status
- Notable transactions

**From OpenBB (if MCP available):**
- Portfolio performance for the week
- Week's market performance (S&P, Nasdaq, Dow)
- Holdings performance (if tracked)
- Any significant market events

**From Khoj (if MCP available):**
- Notes created this week (themes and topics)
- Learning momentum (what you researched)
- Knowledge connections made
- Active learning paths

**From Context Files:**
- `contexts/current-focus.md` - Check stated priorities
- `contexts/profile.md` - Remember goals and values

### 2. Calculate Metrics

**Energy & Mood:**
- Average energy level for the week
- Average mood
- Trend: improving, stable, or declining?
- Patterns: low energy on which days? High energy when?

**Productivity:**
- Tasks completed: [count]
- Notes created: [count]
- Efforts progressed: [list with % progress]

**Financial:**
- Total spent this week
- vs. Budget: [X% of weekly budget]
- Top spending categories
- Unusual transactions

### 3. Reflection Questions

Answer these thoughtfully:

**What went well this week?**
- Top 3 wins (big or small)
- What contributed to these wins?
- What energy or habits led to success?

**What was challenging?**
- Main obstacles or struggles
- Why were these difficult?
- What patterns do you notice?

**What did you learn?**
- Insights about yourself
- Insights about work/life
- Skills developed
- Mistakes made and lessons

**Energy & Time Analysis**
- Where did time actually go?
- Which activities energized you?
- Which drained you?
- Alignment with priorities?

**Relationship Check**
- Who did you connect with?
- Any relationships needing attention?
- Quality vs. quantity of interactions?

**Progress on Goals**
- Movement on quarterly/monthly goals?
- Active Efforts: making progress or stalled?
- What needs to shift?

### 4. Generate Insights

**Pattern Detection:**
Look across Daily Logs for:
- Sleep hours → Energy correlation
- Weather → Mood patterns
- Meeting density → Productivity impact
- Exercise days → Overall wellbeing

**Financial Insights:**
- Spending trends vs. last week
- Budget categories over/under
- Unexpected expenses
- Opportunities to optimize

**Effort Insights:**
- Which efforts got attention?
- Which got ignored?
- Status changes needed (Active → Simmering)?
- New efforts emerging?

### 5. Plan Next Week

**Theme for the Week:**
One-word or phrase describing the focus (e.g., "Momentum", "Recovery", "Building")

**Primary Focus:**
The ONE thing that matters most

**Goals for the Week:**
3-5 specific, achievable outcomes

**Effort Priorities:**
Which Active Efforts get attention?
- Effort 1: [Specific outcome this week]
- Effort 2: [Specific outcome this week]
- Effort 3: [Specific outcome this week]

**Habits to Emphasize:**
Based on this week's learnings, what habits to focus on?
- Continue: [What worked]
- Start: [What to add]
- Stop: [What to drop]

**Time Blocking (Optional):**
If user likes structure:
- Deep work blocks: [When]
- Administrative time: [When]
- Creative time: [When]
- Rest/recharge: [When]

**Financial Plan:**
- Budget for next week
- Planned large expenses
- Savings goal

- `contexts/current-focus.md`:
  - This Week section with new plan
  - Move completed efforts to Done
  - Update effort statuses

## Context Files to Read
- `contexts/current-focus.md` - Compare plan vs. reality
- `contexts/profile.md` - Check alignment with goals
- `contexts/workflows.md` - How user prefers reviews

## Tone & Style

**Match user's review preference from `workflows.md`:**
- Reflective, Analytical, Celebratory, or Balanced
- Depth: Surface-level, Deep, or Varies
- Focus: Wins, Gaps, Both

**Default tone:**
- Honest and constructive
- Celebrate wins genuinely
- Acknowledge challenges without judgment
- Forward-looking and actionable

## Tips

1. **Be thorough but efficient** - Cover all areas without overwhelming
2. **Find meaningful patterns** - Don't just report data, extract insights
3. **Balance reflection and action** - Both looking back and planning ahead
4. **Personalize** - Reference user's goals and values
5. **Be encouraging** - End on a motivating note
